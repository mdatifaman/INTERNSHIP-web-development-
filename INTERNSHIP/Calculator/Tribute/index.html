01 — ABOUT

A Story of Resilience

Sanjay Verma is a survivor of the 1984 Bhopal Gas Tragedy and an activist who has spoken about the continuing struggles of people affected by the disaster.

He was only a few months old when the tragedy occurred. He lost his parents and several siblings. Despite such a difficult beginning, he later became an activist and worked to raise awareness about the needs and rights of Bhopal survivors.
