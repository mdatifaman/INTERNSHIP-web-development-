About Che Guevara

Ernesto "Che" Guevara was an Argentine Marxist revolutionary, physician, author and guerrilla leader. He became one of the most recognizable figures associated with the Cuban Revolution.

Che Guevara is remembered for his strong political beliefs, determination and commitment to revolutionary movements. His image later became an internationally recognized symbol of rebellion and revolution.
